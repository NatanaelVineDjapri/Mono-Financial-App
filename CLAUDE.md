# MONO Finance
 
Premium, charcoal and silver (one silver accent, red only for loss and over-limit), local-first personal finance app for Android. Rupiah (IDR) is the base currency; one optional **Valas** account holds foreign money in one pocket per currency, valued in rupiah. No accounts, no sync. Built with React Native (Expo), TypeScript, Expo SQLite and Expo Router. Network use is limited to two optional features: the AI chat with the user's own Gemini key, and a refresh of exchange rates.
 
The design is finished and lives in a Claude Design canvas (owner's claude.ai account): `https://claude.ai/artifact/2vo4tyC5vNz1HR3uhxEgXX`. Everything needed to build is also in this repo. **Follow the design; if something is unclear or you want to deviate, ask first.**

## Non-negotiable rules
 
1. **Money is an integer in the minor unit of its currency.** Whole rupiah for IDR, cents for USD or SGD, `currencies.minor_factor` says which. No float anywhere. Percentages are basis points (10000 = 100%), rounded half away from zero. Format with the own `rp()`, `money()` and `pct()` in `src/core/money.ts` (spec in DESIGN.md section 16), not `Intl`. Exchange rates are integers too: `rate_e4` is rupiah per 1 major unit times 10.000 (USD at Rp16.250 is `162500000`).
2. **No stored balances.** Account balance, net worth, budget progress, goal progress, savings rate and reports are read from the SQL views. Never write a derived number to a table.
3. **A transfer is one row** (`to_account_id` set, no category). It changes two balances and never net worth, income or expense. A transfer between two different currencies is an **exchange**: the same row stores `amount` (source currency) and `amount_to` (destination), and a transfer inside one Valas account is allowed only between two different pockets.
4. **Rules live in the database.** CHECKs and triggers raise named codes: `future_date`, `before_opening_date`, `category_kind_mismatch`, `insufficient_balance`, `budget_needs_expense_category`, `over_monthly_limit`, `budgets_exist_for_month`, `currency_not_in_account`, `missing_fx_rate`, `pocket_needs_forex_account`, `pocket_not_empty`, `account_type_locked`, `recurring_needs_idr_account`, `forex_account_exists`. The domain layer may pre-check for friendly messages, but the schema is the real guard. Message wording is in DESIGN.md section 14.
5. **Charcoal and silver, with strict jobs.** Colour tokens in `src/theme/tokens.ts`. `accent: '#C7CDD6'` (silver) is the single accent: the centre + button, icon discs, chart series, progress fills. Primary buttons, selected chips and the active tab are solid `#F5F5F5` with dark text, never gradients. `negative: '#FF6B6B'` (red) marks loss, over-limit and errors. `positive: '#4ADE80'` (green) is only for positive money (income, rises, plus-signed amounts), always with the sign. Colour only reinforces a sign, icon or word and is never the only signal. Colour follows meaning, the sign follows arithmetic. Transfers are never coloured. Over-budget bars keep a striped (red) overflow segment plus icon and "Over by" text. No other hue, with two exceptions: the surface of an account card uses the bank's brand palette (the card artwork in `src/components/PhysicalCard`: a per-bank gradient and pattern, an EMV chip and a contactless glyph, all decoration; no card-network badge, since the app does not know a card's network), and a currency's or country's flag keeps its own colours (the discs the Valas and Rates screens and the bank picker draw); the logos of the banks abroad, Krom and Stockbit also keep theirs, on a small white plate; amounts on a card use the card's text colour. No gradients on controls (only the barely visible card fill, the Accounts total card, the account-card artwork and the Overview background image). `#737373` is for disabled and placeholder text only; small captions use `#8A8A8A`.
6. **Type.** IBM Plex Sans for UI and figures (tabular numbers), including hero balances in weight 700. Screen titles and the Overview greeting are IBM Plex Sans 600 too: no serif anywhere (decided by the owner; the boards and `docs/TYPOGRAPHY.md` still show Newsreader). IBM Plex Mono never for money. Fonts are bundled, never fetched.
7. **Accessibility.** Touch targets 44 to 48dp. Support text scale to 200%: rows grow, numbers never truncate. Every chart has an accessibility label and an exact-value table beneath. Never use `Alert` for styled dialogs.
8. **Offline-first.** Only these places may call the network, each with plain `fetch` and a 20 s timeout: `src/features/ai_chat`, `src/features/fx` (exchange-rate refresh) `src/features/location` (the still street map of a transaction's saved location: MapLibre Native drawing OpenFreeMap's free vector tiles, `https://tiles.openfreemap.org/styles/liberty`, no API key; only for a location the person already saved; what leaves the phone is the style and the tiles around the point, an area of a few kilometres, never the exact coordinates, amounts or the place name; MapLibre shows the required attribution; offline, or in a build without the map module, it falls back to the plain grid marked "Offline") and, only if the user connects Gmail, `src/features/import/gmail` (rule 14). Reading notifications for import never uses the network. Enforce with an ESLint rule. The rate refresh runs only when the user taps "Update rates now" or has switched on "Update automatically" (off by default), sends no user data (one GET with no body), and never blocks the app: on failure keep the stored rates. No analytics, no crash SDK, no Firebase.
13. **Valas and rupiah values.** Only a `forex` account has pockets (`account_currencies`); every other account is implicitly IDR. Every transaction stores `amount_idr`, the rupiah value on the day it was recorded, and budgets, reports, income and expense use only that. Account totals and net worth value each pocket at the latest rate (`v_latest_fx`) and show `≈`; when a pocket has no rate, net worth is flagged `incomplete` and the UI says so. Fixed expenses can only use IDR accounts.
14. **Automatic import is optional and never writes on its own.** Two independent sources, both off by default: Android notifications (native listener, no network, no login) and Gmail (Google OAuth on the phone, read-only, no server, no MONO account). Detected items go to a review queue; a transaction is created only when the user taps Simpan, through the same `src/domain` function as a manual one (all guards and `amount_idr` apply). Read only the apps and senders the user picked; never store message text, only parsed fields; keep Gmail tokens in `expo-secure-store` and out of backups. Parsers are one module per institution and are tested with real sample texts: never invent a bank's format. An app without a dedicated parser is read by the general parser and always tagged "Ditebak"; only an app with a dedicated parser is called "Didukung penuh" (see rule 17). Category comes from a local per-merchant memory and keyword rules (empty when unknown, never AI); an ambiguous income/expense is "Perlu dicek" and the user must choose. The app is installed outside Play Store, so Android 13+ "restricted settings" must be explained (board N5).
9. **AI only proposes, and cannot chat.** Nothing is written until the user taps Simpan. The model may only return structured JSON with three outcomes (`create_transactions`, `clarify`, `out_of_scope`); there is no free-text field, and every sentence the user reads is written by the app (fixed sentence per `out_of_scope` reason). Names map to ids by exact or alias match; unknown or missing names become a clarification, never a guess. Drafts pass the same `validate.ts` as the manual form plus an amount cross-check against a local parser (rb, k, jt); a mismatch locks Save until the user chooses. Relative dates are computed in code. No conversation memory, 500 characters per message, one request in flight. Sent to Gemini: typed text, today's date, IDR account names, category names and the sub-category names under them (a sub-category named in the message sets its own category, and the app checks that itself). Never sent: balances, transactions, budgets, goals, backups. The key lives in `expo-secure-store` and is excluded from backups and logs. The model name is one constant in `config.ts` of the AI module. `useAiConfigured()` is true only when a key is saved and the Settings switch is on.
10. **Fixed expenses are templates.** They change no balance, net worth, budget, or report until the user confirms a due date. Confirming writes one normal expense (`source = 'recurring'`) and one `recurring_events` row in one exclusive transaction; skipping writes only the event. Due dates are computed (`v_recurring_due`), not stored; missed months wait, oldest first. Fixed expenses cannot use Valas accounts. Notifications omit amounts when Hide amounts is on. All due counts come from one function (today + overdue); no screen may filter to “today only”.
11. **Restore is all-or-nothing.** One exclusive transaction: set `meta.guards_off` to 1, delete, insert, set it back to 0, `PRAGMA foreign_key_check`, read `v_integrity_issues`. Any error or any returned row rolls back. `backup_log` and `meta` are not exported.
12. **Hidden amounts mode** goes through one gate (`shown()`); every currency figure passes it.
15. **Transaction forms validate in `src/core/tx/validate.ts` (pure, tested) first**; SQL guards are only a backstop and DB error codes go through `mapDbError`. Never write a balance.
16. **Scan struk: OCR di perangkat, gambar tidak disimpan/dikirim, hasil selalu draf.** The receipt scan (`src/features/receipt`, routes `/tx/scan` and `/tx/scan/review`) reads the photo with the on-device ML Kit text recognizer (no network, no account; needs a development build). The picture is only a cache file: it is kept while the review is open, for its thumbnail, and deleted when the review closes, is saved, or is replaced by a new scan, and at once when the scan fails; it is never sent anywhere and never copied out of the cache. Only the parsed fields (shop, date, total candidates) and the recognized lines live in memory (never on disk), and the lines only so that the optional "Rapikan dengan AI" can send them. The result is always a draft on the review screen: a transaction is created only when the user taps Simpan, through the same `saveNewTransaction` as the manual form (`validate.ts` first, all guards, `amount_idr`), with `source = 'scan'`. `parse.ts` is pure and tested on receipt text; when the total is ambiguous it returns every close candidate for the user to pick; a foreign-currency receipt is refused for now. Category comes from simple local rules on the shop name (empty when unknown, never AI); the account defaults to the one used last. A similar expense (same account and amount, within a day) only shows a notice and never blocks saving. **"Rapikan dengan AI"** (`src/features/ai_chat/receiptTidy.ts`, the only network code of the scan, rule 9 applies): optional, needs the saved key and the Settings switch (otherwise the button opens `/ai-setup`), and asks consent every single time. Sent: the recognized text (digit runs of 8 or more blanked, at most 60 lines), rupiah account names, expense category names, today's date. Never sent: the picture, balances, transactions, budgets, goals, backups. The reply is strict JSON with three fields (shop, category, account), no free text; names map to the person's own lists by the chat's matcher (a category by exact, alias or near spelling, an account by exact name only; unknown means not applied). It never touches the total or the date, never overwrites a field the person changed, and saves nothing.
17. **Impor notifikasi: allowlist paket di sisi native, isi pesan tidak pernah disimpan, parser khusus + parser umum, semua hasil lewat tinjauan, tanpa AI; Gmail ditunda.** The listener is a local Expo module (`modules/notification-listener/`, Kotlin). Only packages the user switched on (`import_app_settings.enabled`, which needs a rupiah account) are forwarded to JS; a notification of any other package is dropped in native code and never reaches JS or storage. For an app that is not switched on, only `{package, app name, last time}` is remembered, never a title or text. Message text is never written to the database or to disk (only the parsed fields will be, by the later parsers); the `__DEV__`-only sampler (`/dev/import-sampler`) keeps raw text in memory and is the way to collect real samples. Parsers: a dedicated one per institution (only with real samples, "Didukung penuh") then a general one (always "Ditebak"); everything goes to the review queue and becomes a transaction only on Simpan, through the same domain function as a manual one. Category: local merchant memory and keyword rules, empty when unknown, never AI. No network, no AI. Gmail is postponed: no code, tables or permissions for it. The review queue is built (migration v11: `import_queue`, `import_merchant_memory`; `src/domain/importQueue.ts`; screens `/import/review` and `/import/edit/[id]`): the queue holds parsed fields only, a row becomes a transaction only on Simpan through `validateTx` + `saveNewTransaction(..., 'import')`, rejected and saved rows stay as dedupe markers, and no dedicated parser exists yet (all results are "Ditebak") until real samples are collected.
18. **Status cadangan selalu dihitung dari `backup_log`; ambang peringatan satu konstanta bersama.** The backup strip on More reads the newest `backup_log` row and counts the transactions created or changed since (never a stored or fixed text). "Stale" is `BACKUP_STALE_AFTER_DAYS` (7) in `src/core/backupPolicy.ts`; the backup reminder must import that same constant, never repeat the number. There is ONE "create backup" path, `src/features/backup/createBackup.ts` (export, put the file in a folder the person picks with Android's folder picker, or in the cache and the share sheet for "Bagikan", then the `backup_log` row; closing the folder picker writes and logs nothing): the strip's download button and the Backup screen both call it, and nothing else may write `backup_log` or make a backup file by another route.
 19. **Valas: kantong per mata uang, `amount_idr` dibekukan saat dicatat, kurs terbaru hanya untuk tampilan ≈, kurs hilang tidak pernah dihitung nol, Tukar = transfer antar mata uang, recurring/impor/scan/NFC menolak akun Valas.** Hanya ada satu akun forex (`forex_account_exists`) dan kantongnya bisa mata uang apa pun yang dilayani API kurs (151, `src/core/currencyList.ts`, migrasi v5); uang asing ada di kantongnya (`src/features/valas`, domain tetap di `src/domain`). Tiap transaksi Valas menyimpan `currency` dan `amount_idr` (rupiah pada hari dicatat, setengah menjauhi nol, bilangan bulat); anggaran, laporan, pemasukan dan pengeluaran hanya memakai `amount_idr`, dan kurs yang berubah kemudian tidak menulis ulang apa pun (mengubah catatan membiarkan `amount_idr`, mengubah jumlah, mata uang atau akun membekukannya lagi dari kurs terbaru). Nilai kantong, total akun dan kekayaan bersih memakai kurs terbaru (`v_latest_fx`), selalu dengan "≈"; kantong tanpa kurs bernilai NULL (bukan 0), total ditandai tidak lengkap, dan transaksi baru di mata uang itu diblokir dengan "Belum ada kurs untuk <KODE>. Atur kurs dulu." dan tombol ke `/settings/rates`. Tukar adalah satu baris transfer dengan `amount`, `amount_to` dan `to_currency`; kurs yang tampil dihitung dari kedua jumlah, tidak disimpan; di akun yang sama hanya antara dua kantong berbeda. Kurs: satu GET ke `open.er-api.com/v6/latest/USD` (hasil selain `success` gagal, `rate_e4` = rates.IDR ÷ rates[KODE], riwayat disimpan, cache satu jam, menunggu `time_next_update_utc`, kurs lama tetap saat gagal), "Perbarui otomatis" mati secara default. Obrolan AI, scan struk, impor notifikasi, pengeluaran tetap dan NFC tidak pernah menulis ke akun Valas (`allowForex` hanya dinyalakan form manual).
20. **Target:** progres selalu dihitung dari saldo akun tertaut atau view total aset, maksimal 100%, tanpa saldo turunan tersimpan; laju selalu perkiraan dan dihitung `ceil(sisa / bulan penuh)`. Semua teks UI Target berbahasa Indonesia dan menggunakan bentuk "kamu".
21. **Kategori: diarsipkan dulu, baru bisa dihapus dari daftar arsip (atas permintaan pemilik); catatan dan pengeluaran tetapnya dipindah dulu ke kategori aktif sejenis, anggaran dan sub-kategorinya ikut terhapus (`src/features/categories/removal.ts`). Akun sama: arsipkan dari layar ubah akun (ditolak bila dipakai pengeluaran tetap aktif atau target), akun arsip tetap dihitung di kekayaan bersih, dan saat dihapus catatan serta saldo awalnya digabung ke akun rupiah lain dalam satu transaksi (`src/domain/accounts.ts`); akun Valas tidak bisa. Minimal satu aktif per jenis; jenis terkunci setelah dipakai; pemilih baru hanya menampilkan yang aktif.** Arsip (`archived = 1`) bisa dipulihkan dari Kategori → Diarsipkan. Setiap jenis (pengeluaran, pemasukan) punya minimal satu kategori aktif (`last_active_category`); kategori yang dipakai pengeluaran tetap aktif tidak bisa diarsipkan (`category_in_active_recurring`); jenis tidak bisa diubah begitu transaksi, anggaran, atau pengeluaran tetap memakainya (`category_kind_locked`); nama unik per jenis tanpa membedakan huruf besar kecil, arsip ikut dihitung. Pemilih BARU (transaksi, anggaran, pengeluaran tetap, scan struk, AI chat) hanya menampilkan kategori aktif; mengubah transaksi lama tetap menampilkan kategori arsipnya, dan laporan, riwayat, serta filter menampilkan arsip apa adanya. `ensureMonth` tidak menyalin anggaran kategori arsip. `categories.icon` menyimpan kunci registri (`src/features/categories/icons.ts`), bukan path. Transfer adalah baris sistem virtual, bukan baris tabel. Kode di `src/features/categories/`, penjaga di migrasi v8; semua teks UI Kategori lewat `src/i18n` dalam bentuk "kamu".
22. **Kartu akun adalah hiasan vektor yang ringan.** Seninya (`src/components/PhysicalCard`) adalah SVG statis per bank; aplikasi tidak punya nomor kartu, masa berlaku, maupun jaringan kartu, jadi nomornya selalu topeng polos (`•••• •••• •••• ••••`, tidak pernah angka turunan saldo), tidak ada baris VALID THRU kecuali pemanggil benar-benar punya tanggalnya, dan tidak ada badge jaringan kartu (Visa/Mastercard/GPN), karena app tidak tahu jaringannya. Biaya render dijaga: `PhysicalCard`, latar tiap tema (`theme.Background`, dipakai sebagai komponen, bukan `renderBackground()`), chip, dan glyph kontaktless semuanya `React.memo`, sehingga saldo atau sakelar Sembunyikan nominal hanya menggambar ulang teksnya; daftar banyak kartu memakai `FlatList` (virtualisasi), tidak `ScrollView` + `.map()`.
23. **Analisis: skor kesehatan keuangan dihitung dengan rumus di ponsel, bukan AI, tidak disimpan.** `src/domain/health/` murni (tanpa DB dan React; bobot 25/25/20/15/15, ambang, band, kelayakan, saran dan temuan semuanya konstanta dan fungsi di sana, diuji dengan data Oktober 2026: 83 dan, tanpa anggaran dan pengeluaran tetap, 79). Poin komponen dibulatkan half up dari rasio yang dibulatkan ke persen utuh, skor = jumlah poin yang sudah dibulatkan. Data dibaca dari view yang ada lewat `src/features/analysis/data.ts` (tanpa tabel baru, tanpa menulis apa pun, tidak ikut cadangan); semua kalimat dari templat di `src/i18n` yang disusun `src/features/analysis/copy.ts` (tidak ada kalimat dari model bahasa). Skor, rinciannya, saran, dan kalimatnya tanpa jaringan dan tanpa AI; modul chat AI tidak boleh mengimpor `domain/health` atau `features/analysis`, dan kode skor tidak boleh mengimpor modul AI (aturan ESLint `no-restricted-imports` dan `features/analysis/__tests__/isolation.test.ts`). **Pengecualian satu-satunya, atas permintaan pemilik: kartu "Analisis AI"** di `/analysis`, satu-satunya yang melonggarkan aturan 9 (jawaban JSON ketat dengan teks bebas pendek: ringkasan, maksimal 3 temuan, maksimal 2 saran). Kodenya di `src/features/analysis/ai/` (satu-satunya bagian fitur Analisis yang boleh memakai modul AI) dan `src/features/ai_chat/analysisAi.ts` (satu-satunya kode jaringan, kunci Gemini milik pengguna, klien dan batas waktu yang sama dengan chat). Syarat: kunci tersimpan, saklar AI hidup, dan persetujuan sekali (`settings.ai_analysis_consent`, dicabut dari kartunya); lalu ulasan ditulis otomatis tiap periode yang bisa dinilai dibuka, hasilnya hanya di memori (tidak disimpan, tidak diminta ulang untuk angka yang sama, refresh query tidak memicu permintaan). Dikirim: skor dan lima komponen, pemasukan, pengeluaran, porsi tabungan, pengeluaran tetap, anggaran (kategori, batas, terpakai), pengeluaran per kategori, skor 6 periode, dan 10 pengeluaran terbesar periode itu (tanggal, deskripsi 40 karakter dengan angka panjang, tautan, dan email dibuang, kategori, nominal). Tidak pernah dikirim: nama akun, saldo, target, catatan, struk, cadangan, kunci. Teksnya dibatasi bentuk dan panjangnya (zod ketat), kalimat yang menyebut nominal yang tidak ada di data dibuang, selalu berlabel "Ditulis AI" dengan peringatan bisa keliru, tidak mengubah skor, saldo, anggaran, atau apa pun. Rute `/analysis` dan `/analysis/how`; pintu masuknya baris "Analisis" di Lainnya (plus tautan lama di Ringkasan), pemilih periodenya kalender bulan yang sama dengan Anggaran dan Laporan (`MonthSheet`). Emas hanya untuk skor dan bar, merah hanya di pola bergaris "melebihi"; skor rendah bernada tenang.
24. **Scan kartu e-money (NFC): hanya membaca, hanya kartu e-money tertutup, nomor kartu tidak pernah disimpan, saldo kartu tidak pernah mengubah saldo MONO langsung, tanpa jaringan.** Kode di `src/features/nfc` (pembaca, adaptor per penerbit), `src/domain/cards` (murni: `reconcile`, `fingerprintOf`, `last4Of`, mesin keadaan scan; plus tautan dan penyesuaian), `src/data/repo/cardLinks.ts`, rute `/nfc/scan`, `/nfc/result`, `/nfc/new`; skema migrasi v12 (`card_links`, dua kategori sistem "Penyesuaian saldo", `transactions.source` menerima `'nfc'`). Semua perintah ke kartu lewat satu pintu, `createSafeIo` di `src/features/nfc/transceive.ts`, yang menolak sebelum mengirim apa pun yang bukan perintah BACA (tabel header CLA/INS/P1/P2 yang persis; tidak ada tulis, kredit, debit, format) dan tidak mau memilih aplikasi selain AID e-money yang dideklarasikan adaptor (jadi tidak ada PPSE atau aplikasi EMV, kartu debit dan kredit tidak pernah dibaca). Nomor kartu (PAN) hanya ada di memori selama baca, langsung menjadi 4 digit terakhir dan sidik `sha256(nfc_salt + PAN)` (`readCard.ts`), lalu dibuang: tidak disimpan, tidak di-log, tidak dikirim, dan tidak ada di tipe yang diterima layar. `settings.nfc_salt` dibuat sekali (16 byte acak) dan ikut cadangan; `card_links` ikut cadangan (satu kartu satu akun, hanya akun e-wallet rupiah). Selisih kartu dan akun dicatat sebagai transaksi biasa (`source = 'nfc'`, kategori "Penyesuaian saldo") lewat `validateTx` + `saveNewTransaction`, hanya saat pengguna menekan tombol, dan tidak dicatat bila saldo akun berubah sejak layar digambar. Tidak ada foreground service. Pembacaan hanya berjalan (reader mode) selama layar Scan kartu terbuka. **Satu-satunya pengecualian, atas permintaan pemilik: pendeteksian otomatis.** Plugin `plugins/withNfcLaunch.js` menambah SATU intent filter `android.nfc.action.TECH_DISCOVERED` dengan SATU daftar teknologi `android.nfc.tech.IsoDep` (tanpa TAG/NDEF discovery), sehingga kartu yang ditempel ke ponsel membuka MONO di `/nfc/scan` (`src/features/nfc/NfcTapHost.tsx`, dipasang di layout tab; cold start lewat `getLaunchTagEvent`, saat app berjalan lewat event background tag). Android tidak bisa membedakan kartu e-money dari kartu debit/kredit (sama-sama ISO-DEP), jadi MONO juga ditawari kartu bank yang ditempel; MONO tidak membaca apa pun dari intent itu (tag-nya dibuang, hanya "terjadi atau tidak"), hanya membuka layar Scan kartu, yang membaca lewat `createSafeIo` dan menolak apa pun yang bukan AID e-money yang dideklarasikan. Satu-satunya izin tambahan adalah `android.permission.NFC` (dan `uses-feature` NFC `required=false`, jadi ponsel tanpa NFC tetap bisa memasang aplikasi; tombolnya disembunyikan). Tidak ada kode yang disalin dari proyek GPL; sumber protokol per adaptor ada di komentar kepala berkas dan di `NOTICE`. Hanya Mandiri e-money yang berstatus `stable`; Flazz gen 2, TapCash, dan Brizzi `experimental` (layar memberi catatan), Flazz gen 1 dan JakCard tidak didukung. Di build dev, Dev tools punya sakelar "Pretend card" (kartu tiruan) karena emulator tidak punya NFC.

## Stack and layout
 
Expo (development build, not Expo Go only), `expo-router`, `expo-sqlite`, `@tanstack/react-query` (reads, invalidate after writes), `zustand` (UI-only state), `react-native-reanimated`, `react-native-gesture-handler`, `@shopify/react-native-skia`, `victory-native` (two charts), `expo-font` + `@expo-google-fonts/*`, `expo-video`, `expo-sharing`, `expo-document-picker`, `expo-file-system`, `expo-asset`, `expo-crypto`, `expo-screen-capture`, `expo-notifications`, `expo-secure-store`, `expo-camera` + `expo-image-picker` + `@react-native-ml-kit/text-recognition` (receipt scan, rule 16), `react-native-nfc-manager` (the e-money card scan, rule 24) and `expo-location` (the optional transaction location) and `@maplibre/maplibre-react-native` (its still map, rule 8). Dev: `jest`, `better-sqlite3`, `eslint`. Styling: `StyleSheet` plus the token file. Pin versions with `expo-doctor`.
 
```
app/          expo-router routes only, thin
src/theme  core  data (db, repo, backup)  domain  features  components
assets/       brand, mascot
__tests__/    domain and SQL tests
```
 
Full tree in DESIGN.md section 16. The schema is loaded from a bundled `.sql` asset (add `sql` to Metro `assetExts`) and run with `db.execAsync`. Always `PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON`. Add `.sql` migrations from v2 onwards, each in one transaction, with a safety backup first.
 
Android manifest: Expo declares INTERNET by default. Keep it for the AI chat and the rate refresh only, strip unused permissions with `android.blockedPermissions`, read the merged manifest after prebuild, and check that `expo-notifications` does not require the exact-alarm permission.

## Language
 
**The app UI has two languages: English (the default for now) and Indonesian, chosen in More → Language and kept in `settings.language`** (decided by the owner; it was Indonesian only before). Text is imported from `@/i18n`, which reads `src/i18n/en.ts` or `src/i18n/id.ts` on every call; every sentence exists in both, and `src/i18n/__tests__/parity.test.ts` fails if they drift. The choice in More is switched off for now (`LANGUAGE_CHOICE_ENABLED` in `src/i18n/lang.ts`), so the app is English only until it is set to true; the Indonesian dictionary stays in place and in step. The rest of this section describes the Indonesian wording. Several boards still carry English copy (most core screens); translate it while building. Welcome, AI chat and the fixed-expense confirm sheet are already Indonesian and set the tone: short, direct, "kamu" form.
 
- Keep every user-facing string in the i18n modules, never in a screen: add the sentence to `src/i18n/id.ts` AND `src/i18n/en.ts`, then to the list in `src/i18n/index.ts`, and import it from `@/i18n`. No i18n library. Tests start in Indonesian (`src/i18n/jest-setup.ts`); a test about English sets the language itself.
- Numbers `id_ID` (`Rp1.250.000`, `68,10%`). Dates in Indonesian, short month names (28 Okt 2026).
- Code, route names, SQL, guard codes and file names stay in English. Only text the user sees is Indonesian.
- Proposed glossary (confirm anything doubtful with the owner): Overview = Ringkasan, Transactions = Transaksi, Accounts = Akun, Budgets = Anggaran, More = Lainnya, Goals = Target, Categories = Kategori, Reports = Laporan, Backup and restore = Cadangan dan pemulihan, Settings = Pengaturan, Net worth = Kekayaan bersih, Income = Pemasukan, Expense = Pengeluaran, Transfer = Transfer, Fixed expenses = Pengeluaran tetap, Savings rate = Tingkat tabungan, Opening balance = Saldo awal, Monthly limit = Batas bulanan, Description = Deskripsi, Note = Catatan, Save = Simpan, Cancel = Batal, Delete = Hapus, Undo = Urungkan, Hide amounts = Sembunyikan nominal.
- The destructive "Erase all data?" dialog asks the user to type ERASE in the boards. In Indonesian use "HAPUS" and update the dialog and its validation message together.
- Guard-code messages in DESIGN.md section 14 and DATABASE.md section 4 are in English or are proposals; write the final Indonesian wording in `id.ts`.
## Build order
 
1. **Foundation:** Expo project, TypeScript, `tokens.ts`, `typography.ts`, `money.ts` with tests, fonts, theme.
2. **Database:** schema asset, `open.ts`, repos, domain functions. Port `db/verify_schema.py` to Jest with `better-sqlite3` and reproduce every number above, the Valas section, and all 13 guard codes.
3. **Components:** Button, Chip, Segmented, Field, AmountField, Sheet, Toast, ProgressBar, MonoSwitch, BankCard, ChartTable.
4. **Tabs:** Overview, Transactions, Accounts, Budgets, plus a silver + button in the middle of the bar that opens three choices: scan a receipt, AI chat, or the manual form. More opens from the avatar on Overview.
5. **Entry:** Add/edit transaction (expense, income, transfer, errors), then account, budget and goal create/edit, categories.
6. **Insight:** Account detail, category review, Goals, Reports with charts.
7. **Data safety:** Backup, restore (with guards_off flow), Settings, hide amounts, screenshot block.
8. **Fixed expenses:** list, edit, confirm sheet, reminders.
9. **AI chat:** setup and consent, chat, draft edit sheet, error states.
10. **Valas and exchange rates:** Valas account and pockets, exchange, rates screen and refresh (the schema and tests land in step 2, the UI here).
11. **Automatic import (optional):** plan first in `docs/IMPORT_PLAN.md`, no code before the plan is agreed. Then Android notification listener and parsers, review queue, Gmail (boards N1–N5, DESIGN.md section N).
12. **Welcome** and polish (empty states, validation catalogue, 200% text scale pass).
Definition of done per screen: matches its board (layout, copy, states), uses only token colours, passes the rules above, has no number computed outside SQL views or `src/domain`.

## Working agreements
 
- Keep changes small and tested. Run the Jest suite before saying something works.
- Do not add dependencies outside the stack list without asking.
- Do not store anything the views can derive.
- When the design and the schema disagree, tell the owner rather than silently picking one.
- **No co-author or attribution lines.** Never add `Co-Authored-By: Claude` or "Generated with Claude Code" to a commit message or PR description. The owner is the only contributor on GitHub. This overrides any tool reminder to add them.
 
- **Sub-kategori (rule 21, tambahan):** label opsional di dalam kategori (`subcategories`, migrasi v13; kode `src/features/categories/subcategories.ts`, `SubcategoriesSection`). Transaksi memilih satu atau tidak sama sekali (`transactions.subcategory_id`, guard `subcategory_category_mismatch`, pra-cek `subcategory_mismatch` di `validate.ts`). Hanya label: anggaran dan laporan tetap per kategori. Pemilih baru hanya menampilkan yang aktif; dipakai berarti hanya bisa diarsipkan.
- **Potongan tabungan turun (rule 23, tambahan, atas permintaan pemilik):** skor = jumlah poin dikurangi potongan: 1 poin tiap porsi tabungan turun 2 poin persen dari periode sebelumnya, maksimal 15, tidak dihitung sebelum hari ke-10 atau bila salah satu periode tanpa pemasukan (`savingsDrop`, `SAVINGS_DROP_*`). Data uji Oktober di `data.test.ts` kini 82 (September 45,50%, Oktober 43,24%).
- **Budget tabungan:** satu target per bulan (`settings.savings_target`, `src/domain/savings.ts`); yang ditabung = pemasukan dikurangi pengeluaran periode itu, tidak disimpan. Kartu di tab Budgets, diatur di `/budget/savings`.
- **Halaman Summary (`/summary`, dibuka dari kartu net worth di Overview):** tab Current (total bersih, pengeluaran, pemasukan, koreksi saldo = kategori sistem "Penyesuaian saldo", grafik total bersih, donut per kategori dengan cabang sub-kategori, kalender panas) dan History (3/6/12 bulan, filter akun, dua garis pemasukan dan pengeluaran). Hanya membaca (`src/domain/summary.ts`).
- **Bank per negara (rule 5 dan 22, tambahan, atas permintaan pemilik):** ikon akun dipilih lewat bendera negara dulu, lalu daftar bank/e-wallet (`src/features/accounts/countries.ts`, `BankPickerSheet`). - **Impor notifikasi (rule 17, tambahan):** layanan pembaca disambungkan lagi setelah app di-update (receiver `MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED`, lalu `restartService` bila `requestRebind` tidak cukup) dan membaca notifikasi yang masih di bar saat tersambung. Cadangan ikut `debts`.
